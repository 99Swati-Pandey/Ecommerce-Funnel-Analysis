# 🛒 E-commerce Funnel Analysis

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes user behavior across an e-commerce funnel to identify key drop-off points and uncover actionable insights to improve conversion rates.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To diagnose where and why users drop off in the purchase journey and determine whether the issue is driven by product, pricing, user experience, or behavioral factors.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics
- **45% drop-off** from Add-to-Cart → Checkout  
- **~55% conversion rate** across country, device, and traffic source  
- **~53 minutes average time** from cart to checkout  

---

## 🔍 Key Findings
- Conversion rates are **consistent across segments**, indicating no geographic, device, or channel-specific issue  
- **Pricing has no significant impact** on drop-off behavior  
- Users show **high engagement (multi-item carts, longer sessions)** but still fail to convert  
- Cart is frequently used as a **wishlist or comparison tool** rather than a purchase step  

---

## 🧠 Root Cause
The primary driver of drop-off is **low purchase intent and lack of urgency**, not system friction.  
Users delay decisions and do not feel compelled to complete the purchase immediately.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Introduce **urgency triggers** (low-stock alerts, countdown timers)  
- Provide **checkout-stage incentives** (discounts, free shipping thresholds)  
- Implement **cart abandonment recovery** (email reminders, retargeting)  
- Build **trust signals** (reviews, return policies, secure payment badges)  
- Simplify checkout with **fewer steps and “Buy Now” options**
